What AI Storage Fee Detection Identifies

Rate Increase Violations

Storage facilities raise rates regularly. AI detects rate increases that exceed contractual caps of 5 to 10 percent, increases more frequently than allowed by contract, and promotional first-month rates that automatically escalate to much higher rates without clear disclosure.

Administrative Fee Inflation

Storage facilities charge fees for administrative tasks. AI detects administrative fees for processing rent payments that exceed reasonable costs, move-out cleaning fees for units left in good condition, and lock cutting fees that exceed the actual cost of replacing a lock.

Insurance Markups

Storage facilities require or offer insurance. AI detects monthly insurance premiums of $10-$30 for coverage worth $15-$50 per year from an independent provider, insurance requirements that duplicate existing homeowners or renters insurance, and forced placement insurance at inflated rates when the tenant fails to provide proof of existing coverage.

Late Fee Overcharges

Late fees are regulated by state law. AI detects late fees exceeding state statutory maximums, late fees assessed before the grace period expires, and late fees applied to amounts that include administrative fees rather than just the base rent.
