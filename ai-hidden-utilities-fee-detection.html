Utility Fee Categories

Electric and Gas

Electric and gas bills include distribution charges, transmission surcharges, fuel adjustment fees, and regulatory recovery costs. Distribution charges that exceed approved per-unit rates filed with public utility commissions are a common area of concern.

Water and Sewer

Water bills include usage charges, base fees, sewer charges based on water usage, and infrastructure surcharges. Sewer charge calculation errors where the percentage applied exceeds approved rates are a potential area of concern.

Waste Management

Trash and recycling service bills include base service fees, container rental charges, disposal surcharges, and fuel adjustment fees. Container rental fees for bins not provided and fuel surcharges exceeding actual cost increases are potential issues.
