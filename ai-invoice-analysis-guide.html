Common Invoice Errors AI Detects

Duplicate Charges and Invoices

Duplicate charges are among the most common and costly invoice errors. A vendor may submit the same invoice through multiple channels. A single service may be billed by multiple departments. AI detects duplicates by matching invoice numbers, dates, amounts, vendor names, and service descriptions across all submitted invoices. The AI at DetectHiddenFees.com achieves 97 percent detection accuracy for duplicate charges compared to approximately 35 percent for manual review. Average financial impact: $500 to $5,000 per occurrence.

Unauthorized Add-Ons and Upcharges

Vendors frequently add charges for services not included in the original agreement including expedited shipping, handling fees, administrative fees, and minimum order surcharges. AI identifies these by comparing each line item against the original purchase order or service agreement. Unauthorized add-ons are detected at 94 percent accuracy versus 25 percent for manual review. Average financial impact: $200 to $2,000 per occurrence.

Above-Market Pricing

AI compares each unit price against current market rates for comparable products and services. Pricing that exceeds reasonable market ranges is flagged immediately. This is critical for businesses that purchase the same items repeatedly, as vendors may gradually increase prices without notice. AI detects above-market pricing at 92 percent accuracy versus 30 percent for manual review. Financial impact: 10 to 30 percent of total invoice value.

Mathematical and Calculation Errors

Invoice calculations including subtotals, taxes, discounts, and totals are automatically verified. Mathematical errors occur in approximately 3 to 5 percent of all invoices. AI detects these with 99 percent accuracy. Average financial impact: $50 to $500 per occurrence.
