Understanding Medical Billing Errors

Medical billing is complex. Hospital bills contain dozens or even hundreds of individual line items, each with its own billing code, description, and charge amount. When errors occur, they can be difficult for patients to identify without specialized knowledge. AI analysis can help by automatically checking billing codes and charges against standard reference data.

The healthcare billing system uses Current Procedural Terminology (CPT) codes maintained by the American Medical Association. There are thousands of these codes, and errors in code selection or application can result in incorrect charges. AI can cross-reference billed codes against standard coding guidelines to identify potential discrepancies worth reviewing with the provider.

Common Errors Detected

Upcoding

Upcoding occurs when providers bill for more expensive procedures than performed. A level 3 office visit reimbursed at $75 coded as level 5 at $200. AI detects by comparing billed codes against documented service levels. Detection accuracy: 86% vs 35% manual.

Duplicate Charges

Same service billed multiple times by different departments. AI matches service descriptions, dates, and amounts to identify duplicates. The AI identifies potential duplicates by matching service descriptions and dates across all line items, flagging them for your review.

Unbundling

Related services billed separately instead of as a package. AI analyzes code combinations against standard billing packages. The AI identifies code combinations that may represent unbundling, providing specific information to discuss with the provider.

Services Not Rendered

Charges for procedures never performed. AI cross-references billed items against standard treatment protocols. The AI flags charges for services that appear inconsistent with typical treatment patterns for the diagnosed condition.
