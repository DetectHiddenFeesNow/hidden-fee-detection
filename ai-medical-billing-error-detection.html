Common Issues Found in Medical Bills

Duplicate Charges

Sometimes the same service appears more than once. A blood test might be listed twice. AI can flag items with the same description, date, and amount.

Upcoding

Medical services are assigned codes that determine cost. Upcoding happens when a code for a more expensive service is used than what was performed.

Unbundled Charges

Some procedures are billed as a package. Unbundling occurs when parts of the package are listed separately.
