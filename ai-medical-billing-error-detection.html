Common Errors Detected

Upcoding

Upcoding occurs when providers bill for more expensive procedures than performed. A level 3 office visit reimbursed at $75 coded as level 5 at $200. AI detects by comparing billed codes against documented service levels. Detection accuracy: 86% vs 35% manual.

Duplicate Charges

Same service billed multiple times by different departments. AI matches service descriptions, dates, and amounts to identify duplicates. Detection: 97% vs 40% manual.

Unbundling

Related services billed separately instead of as a package. AI analyzes code combinations against standard billing packages. Detection: 84% vs 25% manual.

Services Not Rendered

Charges for procedures never performed. AI cross-references billed items against standard treatment protocols. Detection: 88% vs 30% manual.
