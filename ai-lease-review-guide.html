Red Flags to Watch For

  • Charges that are not explained or are described in vague terms
  • Fees that appear to duplicate other listed charges
  • Contracts with automatic renewal without adequate cancellation notice
  • Prices that increase after the initial promotional period without notice
  • Service fees that are added to charges that should be included in the base price

Common Misconceptions

"The final bill will match the initial quote." Additional fees may be added for services or items that were not included in the initial quote.

"All charges will be clearly explained." Some charges may appear on invoices or billing statements without clear descriptions.
