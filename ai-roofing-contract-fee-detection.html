What AI Roofing Fee Detection Identifies

Material Markups Above Wholesale

Roofing contractors mark up materials 30 to 60 percent as standard. AI detects markups of 100 to 300 percent above wholesale, shingles quoted at premium tier prices but lower tier specified in the fine print, and underlayment, flashing, and ventilation materials at 3 to 5 times wholesale cost.

Labor Overcharges

Roofing labor rates vary by region. AI detects labor rates that exceed regional averages by 30 to 50 percent, crew size misrepresentation where the estimate assumes a larger crew than is actually used, and tear-off charges that are listed separately when removal is included in the standard installation price.

Warranty Manipulation

Manufacturers provide standard warranties of 25 to 50 years. AI detects extended warranty plans that duplicate manufacturer coverage, workmanship warranties that exclude common failure modes, and shingle grade substitutions where the quote specifies premium shingles but standard shingles are installed.

Storm Chaser Pricing

Out-of-town contractors following severe weather events charge inflated prices. AI detects pricing that is 50 to 100 percent above local market averages, high-pressure sales tactics with same-day decision discounts that aren't actually discounts, and insurance claim assignment clauses that give the contractor control over claim payments.

Permit Fee Padding

Roofing permits cost $50 to $500 depending on jurisdiction. AI detects permit fees that are padded 200 to 400 percent above actual city or county permit costs, multiple permit fees for a single project, and permit fees that are charged but never actually obtained.
