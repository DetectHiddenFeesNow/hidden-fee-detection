What AI Food Delivery Fee Detection Identifies

Inflated Service Fees

Food delivery platforms charge service fees that vary widely by restaurant, location, and order size. AI detection identifies service fees that exceed the platform's stated percentage, fees calculated on amounts that include tax or tip when they should be calculated on the subtotal only, and service fees that increase during peak hours beyond disclosed ranges. Many platforms charge 10 to 20 percent service fees, but AI analysis reveals that some orders have fees reaching 30 percent or more when calculated as a percentage of the food cost.

Menu Price Markups

Delivery platforms allow restaurants to set higher prices on their platform than in-store. AI detection identifies menu item prices on delivery receipts that exceed the restaurant's standard menu prices by comparing against known pricing data. Some restaurants increase prices by 15 to 40 percent on delivery platforms to offset platform commissions. AI flags items where the markup exceeds the typical range for that restaurant category.

Delivery Fee Without Free Delivery Eligibility

Many platforms offer free delivery above certain order thresholds or as part of subscription programs. AI detects delivery fees charged when the order qualifies for free delivery based on the platform's stated threshold, double delivery fees on split orders, and delivery fees on pickup orders that include a delivery fee in error.

Unauthorized Gratuity Additions

Some restaurants and delivery platforms add automatic gratuity for large orders but occasionally apply it incorrectly. AI detects automatic gratuity applied to small orders below the stated threshold, gratuity calculated on tax or fees instead of the food subtotal, and double gratuity where a tip is charged in the app and also added manually by the restaurant.

Duplicate Charges

AI detection identifies transaction processing that results in duplicate authorizations or charges on the same order. These errors are most common when an order is placed, fails, and then is re-placed successfully. The duplicate is often automatically refunded but not always. AI flags orders where multiple charges appear for the same date, restaurant, and amount pattern.

Incorrect Tax Calculations

Food delivery tax rates vary by jurisdiction and can include preparation tax, delivery tax, and local surcharges. AI detects tax amounts that exceed the applicable rate for the delivery location, taxes applied to non-taxable items such as tips, and multiple layers of taxation that double-count certain fees. Prepared food taxes in some cities add 1 to 4 percent beyond standard sales tax, and AI verifies that only applicable taxes are charged.

Bag and Packaging Fees

Some jurisdictions require restaurants to charge for bags or packaging. AI detects bag fees that exceed local legal limits, packaging fees charged per item rather than per bag, and bundled packaging fees that are not itemized. These fees are typically $0.10 to $0.50 per bag but are sometimes inflated to $1 or more per item.

Subscription Program Fee Leakage

Delivery platforms offer subscription programs such as DashPass and Uber One that provide free delivery and reduced service fees. AI detects subscription benefits that are not applied to eligible orders, duplicate subscription charges, and promotional trial periods that convert to full price without adequate disclosure. The average consumer with multiple delivery subscriptions spends $240 per year on these programs.

Types of Food and Dining Documents Reviewed

Food Delivery App Receipts

Digital receipts from DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub, Postmates, and other platforms contain itemized fees that are often incomplete or misleading. AI extracts every line item and identifies service fee percentages that exceed disclosed rates, menu price markups, and delivery fee eligibility errors.

Restaurant Dining Bills

Restaurant bills include subtotals, tax, tip, and sometimes automatic gratuity, service charges, and surcharges. AI detects mandatory service charges that are not disclosed on the menu, credit card processing fees added to bills, and incorrect tax applications. Some restaurants add health insurance surcharges, living wage adjustments, and kitchen appreciation fees that are not clearly disclosed.

Catering and Large Order Invoices

Catering invoices include multiple line items for food, delivery, setup, service staff, equipment rental, and cleanup. AI detects duplicate charges, staff hour inflation, equipment charges for items that were not provided, and delivery charges that exceed reasonable rates for the distance and order size.

Meal Kit Subscription Bills

Meal kit services such as HelloFresh, Blue Apron, and Home Chef charge weekly or bi-weekly subscription fees plus shipping. AI detects shipping fees that exceed disclosed amounts, charged weeks that skip without delivery, and price increases that are not communicated as required by terms of service.

Restaurant Gift Cards

Gift card purchases come with activation fees, maintenance fees, and expiration policies. AI detects post-purchase fees that reduce card value, inactivity fees applied before the legally permitted dormancy period, and expiration dates that conflict with state laws prohibiting gift card expiration.

Event and Venue Catering Contracts

Event catering contracts include food and beverage minimums, service charges, gratuity policies, and cancellation terms. AI detects service charges calculated on pre-tax amounts when the contract specifies post-tax calculation, minimums that are not clearly defined, and cancellation penalties that exceed reasonable estimates of actual damages.

Common Misconceptions

"The advertised price is the total cost." Many services add fees, surcharges, and taxes that are not included in the advertised price. The final cost may be significantly higher than the initial quote.

"I can cancel at any time without penalty." Many contracts have minimum terms, cancellation fees, or notice periods that make it costly or difficult to cancel before the end of the term.

"My insurance or warranty covers everything." Insurance policies and warranty contracts typically have exclusions, deductibles, and coverage limits. Understanding what is not covered is as important as understanding what is covered.

"All providers charge similar prices." Pricing can vary significantly between providers for the same service. Shopping around and comparing quotes is recommended.
