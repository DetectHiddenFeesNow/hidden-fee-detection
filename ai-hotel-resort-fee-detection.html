What AI Hotel Fee Detection Identifies

Resort Fee Inflation

Mandatory resort fees of $15 to $60 per night are added to room rates. AI detects resort fees that are not disclosed in the advertised room rate, fees that exceed the amount stated at booking, and resort fees charged at properties that do not offer the amenities typically covered by such fees.

Parking Charge Inflation

Hotel parking fees range from $10 to $60 per night. AI detects parking charges that exceed the hotel's posted daily rate, valet parking automatically charged when self-parking was used, and parking fees for electric vehicle charging that are not disclosed.

Early Check-In and Late Checkout Fees

Hotels charge for early arrival or late departure. AI detects early check-in fees when the room was available and no early arrival was requested, late checkout fees that exceed the hourly rate stated at check-in, and full-day charges for checkout only an hour past the standard time.

Minibar and Snack Markups

Hotel minibar charges are 300 to 500 percent above retail. AI detects minibar charges for items that were not consumed, restocking fees for items inspected but untouched, and snack charges at rates exceeding the in-room price list.

Incidental Hold Overcharges

Hotels place incidental holds on credit cards at check-in. AI detects holds that are 2 to 3 times the stated incidental deposit policy, holds that remain on cards for days after checkout despite immediate release policies, and holds that are converted to charges without itemized backup.
