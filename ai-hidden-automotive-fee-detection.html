Automotive Fee Categories

Auto Repair

Vehicle repair invoices commonly have parts markups, labor rate inflation, unnecessary repairs, and diagnostic fee padding. Parts may be marked up above manufacturer suggested retail prices, and flat-rate labor times may exceed manufacturer specifications.

Car Rental

Rental agreements include collision damage waiver upsells that duplicate existing credit card coverage, fuel service overcharges, toll fee processing markups, and damage claim inflation. Add-ons can significantly increase the effective daily rate above the advertised price.

Vehicle Warranties

Extended warranty contracts have exclusion clauses for commonly needed repairs, deductible structures that apply per component rather than per visit, and administrative fees deducted from claims. Coverage tiers may have names that imply broader protection than the contract delivers.

RV and Camper Purchases

RV dealer contracts include prep fees, extended warranty exclusions for slide-outs and awnings, and financing rate manipulation. The RV market has unique fee structures that differ from standard auto purchases.

Car Dealer Financing

Dealership financing may include rate markup programs, GAP insurance inflation, extended warranty bundling, and document fee overcharges. These fees can add thousands to the total purchase price.

Towing Services

Towing invoices have hook-up fee double charging, mileage overcharges, storage fee inflation, and after-hours surcharge misapplication. Towing rates are regulated by local ordinances in many jurisdictions.
