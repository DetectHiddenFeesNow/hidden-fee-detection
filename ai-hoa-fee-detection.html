What AI HOA Fee Detection Identifies

Special Assessment Manipulation

Special assessments fund major repairs beyond the regular budget. AI detects assessments for projects that should be covered by the reserve fund included in regular dues, assessments voted on without the proper homeowner approval percentage, and amounts padded 20-40 percent above actual project costs.

Fine Inflation

HOAs impose fines for rule violations. AI detects fines that exceed the maximum amount specified in the CC&Rs, fines for violations that are not actually defined in the governing documents, and escalation fees for unpaid fines that compound faster than allowed by state law.

Transfer and Move-In Fee Overcharges

HOAs charge fees when properties change ownership. AI detects transfer fees of $300-$500 that exceed the actual cost of updating records, move-in fees that duplicate the transfer fee, and move-out fees for tenants vacating rental properties when the fee was already collected at move-in.

Reserve Fund Underfunding

HOAs must maintain reserve funds for major repairs. AI detects reserve contributions that are below the amount recommended by the reserve study, deferred maintenance that will require large special assessments, and reserve fund transfers for non-capital purposes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most common hidden fee in pest control contracts?

Chemical markups and treatment frequency inflation where monthly treatments are scheduled when quarterly treatments would be standard for the pest type and region.

Can AI detect HVAC equipment markups?

Yes. AI can analyze HVAC installation contracts to identify equipment pricing above wholesale markups, warranty duplication, and refrigerant overcharges.

What are storage unit rate increase limits?

Storage contracts specify rate increase terms that vary by facility. Some contracts cap increases at a specific percentage, while others do not have caps.

How can I challenge a furniture restocking fee?

Restocking fees should be disclosed in the purchase terms. Fees that were not disclosed or that exceed the disclosed amount can be disputed with the merchant.

What is a home warranty service call fee?

A service call or trip fee is charged each time a technician visits. Per-appliance fees apply if multiple appliances are serviced during the same visit under some warranty plans.

What are common renovation contract pitfalls?

Double-counted overhead where profit is applied to costs that already include markup, change order pricing above base contract rates, and permit fee padding above actual government costs are areas to review.
