What AI Travel Insurance Fee Detection Identifies

Pre-Existing Condition Exclusion Overreach

Travel insurance excludes pre-existing conditions within a lookback period. AI detects lookback periods of 180 days or more when 60 days is standard, exclusion language that applies to conditions a reasonable person would not consider relevant, and waiver requirements that are not disclosed until after purchase.

Cancel for Any Reason Manipulation

CFAR coverage promises 50 to 75 percent reimbursement. AI detects CFAR reimbursement calculated after subtracting deductibles, administrative fees, and non-refundable portions that reduce the payout far below advertised percentages, time windows for CFAR cancellation that are 10 to 14 days after purchase rather than the standard 48 hours, and CFAR availability that is not offered on the lowest fare classes.

Claim Denial Errors

Travel insurance claims are denied for various reasons. AI detects denials based on exclusions that do not match the actual circumstance, denials for late filing when the policy allows electronic filing with timestamps, and denials for insufficient documentation when the policy does not specify what documentation is required.

Premium Markups

Travel insurance premiums vary dramatically. AI detects premiums that exceed comparable policies by 50 to 100 percent for identical coverage, bundling of unnecessary add-ons like rental car coverage and flight accident insurance, and non-refundable premium policies where the entire premium is forfeited if the trip is cancelled even when the policy was never used.

Secondary Coverage Clauses

Travel insurance often pays secondary to other coverage. AI detects secondary clauses that require exhausting all other insurance before paying, effectively making the travel insurance valueless for travelers with health insurance and credit card travel benefits, and reimbursement limits that apply after deducting amounts paid by other insurance even when the other insurance has different covered benefits.

FAQ

What is the most common hidden fee in pest control contracts?

Chemical markups and treatment frequency inflation where monthly treatments are scheduled when quarterly treatments would be standard for the pest type and region.

Can AI detect HVAC equipment markups?

Yes. AI can analyze HVAC installation contracts to identify equipment pricing above wholesale markups, warranty duplication, and refrigerant overcharges.

What are storage unit rate increase limits?

Storage contracts specify rate increase terms that vary by facility. Some contracts cap increases at a specific percentage, while others do not have caps.

How can I challenge a furniture restocking fee?

Restocking fees should be disclosed in the purchase terms. Fees that were not disclosed or that exceed the disclosed amount can be disputed with the merchant.

What is a home warranty service call fee?

A service call or trip fee is charged each time a technician visits. Per-appliance fees apply if multiple appliances are serviced during the same visit under some warranty plans.

What are common renovation contract pitfalls?

Double-counted overhead where profit is applied to costs that already include markup, change order pricing above base contract rates, and permit fee padding above actual government costs are areas to review.
