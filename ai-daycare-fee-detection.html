What AI Daycare Fee Detection Identifies

Registration Fee Overcharges

Annual registration fees of $50 to $200 are charged every year but cover only the first year's administrative overhead. AI detects registration fees charged annually despite the contract stating it covers a one-time cost, registration fee increases that are not disclosed in the original fee schedule, and multiple registration fees for siblings at higher per-child rates.

Late Pickup Penalty Errors

Late pickup fees are charged per minute after closing time. AI detects late pickup penalties calculated at rates exceeding the daycare's stated policy, grace periods that are not honored, and late fees applied on days when the daycare closed early for holidays or training without notice.

Supply and Material Fee Markups

Supply fees cover art materials, diapers, wipes, and snacks. AI detects supply fees that are charged in addition to tuition that already includes these materials, mandatory supply list purchases from specific vendors at marked up prices, and supply fees that increase mid-year without notice.

Activity Fee Duplication

Field trips and special activities incur separate fees. AI detects activity fees for trips that were canceled but not refunded, activity fees that duplicate enrichment programs already included in the base tuition, and multiple fees for the same activity charged to different family accounts.

Holiday and Closure Credit Errors

Daycares close for holidays and training days. AI detects tuition charged in full for months with multiple closure days when the contract provides for credits, holiday schedules that differ from the published calendar without adjusting tuition, and closures for inclement weather that are not credited.

Deposit and Security Fund Retention

Daycares hold deposits for damage or unpaid fees. AI detects deposits retained beyond the contractually specified period after withdrawal, deductions from deposits without itemized receipts, and deposits applied to charges that were not disclosed in the enrollment agreement.
