What AI Legal Fee Detection Identifies

Block Billing

Block billing groups multiple tasks into a single time entry at the highest applicable rate. AI detects entries containing 3 or more unrelated tasks billed as one unit, entries where the total time exceeds what is reasonable for the described tasks, and entries that combine legal work with administrative tasks at the legal rate.

Hourly Rate Inflation

Attorney hourly rates are specified in the engagement letter. AI detects time billed at rates higher than the agreed rate, annual rate increases that exceed the contractual maximum or were not disclosed as required, and senior partner rates applied to work actually performed by associates or paralegals.

Excessive Administrative Charges

Legal bills include charges for photocopying, faxes, postage, and document retrieval. AI detects photocopying charges of $0.25 to $0.50 per page when actual cost is $0.05 to $0.10, fax transmission charges at $1 to $3 per page when digital transmission is essentially free, online research charges at full retail when the firm has a flat-rate subscription, and travel time billed at the full hourly rate when the engagement letter specifies a reduced rate.

Task Duplication

Multiple attorneys may bill for the same event. AI detects multiple attorneys billing for the same deposition or hearing at full rates, internal conferences where 2 or more attorneys bill for discussing the same issue, and document review where multiple attorneys each bill for reading the same document.

Retainer Retention Errors

Retainers are advance payments for legal services. AI detects retainers that are retained beyond the amount actually earned, retainer replenishment clauses that require payment before services are rendered, and non-refundable retainer provisions that may violate state ethics rules in certain jurisdictions.

Common Misconceptions

"The advertised price is the total cost." Many services add fees, surcharges, and taxes that are not included in the advertised price. The final cost may be significantly higher than the initial quote.

"I can cancel at any time without penalty." Many contracts have minimum terms, cancellation fees, or notice periods that make it costly or difficult to cancel before the end of the term.

"My insurance or warranty covers everything." Insurance policies and warranty contracts typically have exclusions, deductibles, and coverage limits. Understanding what is not covered is as important as understanding what is covered.

"All providers charge similar prices." Pricing can vary significantly between providers for the same service. Shopping around and comparing quotes is recommended.
