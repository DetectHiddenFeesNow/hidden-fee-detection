4. Medical Billing Errors

Duplicate charges appear when the same service is billed multiple times by different departments within the same hospital. Unbundling occurs when related services that should be billed together under a single comprehensive code are itemized separately, increasing the total reimbursement. The average overcharge is $1,200 per hospital visit, with major procedures seeing $5,000 to $20,000 in identified errors.
