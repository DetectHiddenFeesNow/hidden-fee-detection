Real Estate Fee Categories

Home Purchase and Mortgage

Real estate purchase agreements involve commission structures, closing costs, and transfer fees. Mortgage refinance documents conceal origination fee increases, rate lock extension charges, and yield spread premium manipulation. Broker fee stacking and force-placed insurance add hidden costs.

Homeowners Association Fees

HOA governing documents specify fee structures, special assessment rules, and fine schedules. Issues include special assessments for routine maintenance, fines exceeding CC&R limits, and transfer fee overcharges.

Property Taxes

Property tax appeal services charge contingency fees of 25-50 percent of savings. Some contracts define savings broadly to include reductions that would have occurred automatically.

Moving Services

Moving company contracts involve weight re-weigh manipulation, stair fees, long carry surcharges, and packing material markups. The moving industry has one of the highest complaint rates of any consumer service.

Home Renovation

Renovation contracts may have double-counted overhead, material price inflation, change order gouging, and permit fee padding. Contractors may apply profit and overhead to costs that already include their markup.

Common Misconceptions About Real Estate Fees

"The Closing Disclosure shows the final, fixed costs." Some fees can change between the Closing Disclosure and actual closing, though TRID rules limit how much certain fees can increase without re-disclosure. Reviewing both documents helps identify changes.

"HOA fees only cover maintenance and repairs." HOA fees may also fund reserves, management salaries, insurance, utilities, and amenities. Understanding the budget breakdown helps homeowners evaluate whether fees are reasonable.

"Moving company estimates are binding." Non-binding estimates can change based on actual weight and services required. Binding estimates lock in the price but may not include all potential charges. Understanding which type of estimate you have is important.

What is the most common hidden fee in real estate transactions?

Recording fee markups above actual government rates are among the most common issues found in closing disclosures. Title fee bundling and escrow padding are also frequently identified.

Can AI detect hidden fees in HOA documents?

Yes. AI can analyze HOA governing documents to identify special assessment provisions, fine schedules, and transfer fee policies that may result in unexpected costs for homeowners.

How can I avoid moving company overcharges?

Get binding estimates in writing, understand what is included, ask about potential additional charges, and verify the company's complaint history with the Better Business Bureau before signing a contract.
