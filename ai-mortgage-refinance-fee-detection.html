What AI Mortgage Refinance Fee Detection Identifies

Hidden Origination Fee Increases

Lenders provide an initial Loan Estimate but may increase origination fees before closing. AI detects origination fee increases that exceed the 10 percent tolerance threshold for the Loan Estimate category, origination points that increase without a corresponding rate improvement, and lender credits that decrease from the estimate without proper re-disclosure. TRID rules require lenders to re-disclose if certain fees increase beyond tolerance limits, and AI identifies violations that entitle borrowers to fee refunds.

Rate Lock Extension Fee Overcharges

Rate lock agreements specify an interest rate for a set period. AI detects rate lock extension fees that exceed the lender's published extension policy, extension fees quoted verbally that differ from the written fee schedule, and rate lock periods that expire because the lender failed to process the application on time forcing the borrower to pay an extension. Extension fees can range from 0.125 percent to 0.5 percent of the loan amount for each 15-day extension period.

Prepayment Penalties on Existing Mortgage

While most conventional mortgages do not have prepayment penalties, some refinances involve paying off an existing loan with a prepayment penalty clause. AI detects prepayment penalties that were not disclosed in the refinance savings analysis, penalties calculated on the wrong loan balance, and FHA or USDA loan assumption fees that function as prepayment penalties. Prepayment penalties can range from 1 to 3 percent of the loan balance.

Appraisal Fee Inflation

Lenders charge appraisal fees on the Closing Disclosure that often exceed the actual amount paid to the appraisal company. AI detects appraisal fees that exceed typical charges for the property type and location, appraisal fees that include administrative or processing add-ons not disclosed as separate charges, and appraisal waiver fees charged when no physical appraisal was performed. The typical appraisal fee inflated by $75 to $150 per refinance.

Yield Spread Premium Manipulation

When borrowers accept a higher interest rate in exchange for lender credits to reduce closing costs, the lender earns a yield spread premium from the secondary market investor. AI detects cases where the borrower accepts a higher rate that should generate sufficient lender credits to cover all costs but the lender also charges full origination fees without applying the premium. This double-dipping adds $1,000 to $5,000 to refinance costs.

Duplicate Underwriting and Processing Fees

Refinance transactions often include underwriting fees, processing fees, and administrative fees that duplicate services already covered by the origination charge. AI detects duplicate fee structures where the origination fee, underwriting fee, processing fee, and administrative fee each appear as separate line items when some should be bundled. Some lenders charge 3 to 5 separate fees for services that are typically included in a single origination fee.

Tax Service Fee Overcharges

Tax service fees cover the cost of monitoring property tax payments during the loan term. AI detects tax service fees that exceed the lender's actual cost, tax service fees on refinances where the existing tax service can be transferred without charge, and flood certification fees that are charged multiple times for the same property. These fees typically cost lenders $5 to $20 but borrowers may be charged $75 to $125.

Escrow Waiver and Setup Fees

Lenders may allow borrowers to waive escrow accounts if they pay a fee. AI detects escrow waiver fees that exceed reasonable amounts, escrow setup fees on refinances where the existing escrow can be transferred, and escrow analysis fees charged annually without disclosure in the original terms. Escrow waiver fees range from $300 to $500 but are sometimes inflated to $750 or more.
