What AI HVAC Fee Detection Identifies

Equipment Markups Above Wholesale

HVAC contractors mark up equipment 50 to 100 percent above wholesale as standard. AI detects markups of 150 to 300 percent where the contractor charges more than double the typical markup, equipment priced at retail or above with no wholesale discount passed to the customer, and brand substitutions where a lower-tier brand is quoted at premium brand pricing.

Installation Surcharge Inflation

Installation quotes include permits, materials, and labor. AI detects permit fees that exceed actual city or county permit costs by 200 to 400 percent, material charges for items like line sets and ductwork at 3 to 5 times wholesale cost, and dump fees for old equipment that exceed actual disposal costs.

Warranty Manipulation

Manufacturers warranty compressors and parts for 10 years. AI detects extended warranty plans sold at $500 to $2,000 that duplicate the manufacturer warranty, labor warranty plans that exclude the labor costs for common repairs, and registration fees that the contractor charges but the manufacturer provides free online registration.

Refrigerant Price Gouging

R-410A refrigerant costs $50 to $150 per pound for contractors wholesale. AI detects refrigerant charges of $75 to $150 per pound to the customer when wholesale is $3 to $8 per pound for common refrigerants, refrigerant costs quoted as a flat fee that far exceeds the amount needed for the specific system, and unnecessary full-system refrigerant replacement when only a small recharge is needed.

Service Call and Diagnostic Fee Inflation

Service call fees cover the technician arriving at the home. AI detects diagnostic fees of $80 to $150 that are charged in addition to the service call fee when the diagnostic is included in the call, overtime rates applied during normal business hours, and trip charges for follow-up visits that resulted from incomplete initial service.

FAQ

What is the most common hidden fee in pest control contracts?

Chemical markups and treatment frequency inflation where monthly treatments are scheduled when quarterly treatments would be standard for the pest type and region.

Can AI detect HVAC equipment markups?

Yes. AI can analyze HVAC installation contracts to identify equipment pricing above wholesale markups, warranty duplication, and refrigerant overcharges.

What are storage unit rate increase limits?

Storage contracts specify rate increase terms that vary by facility. Some contracts cap increases at a specific percentage, while others do not have caps.

How can I challenge a furniture restocking fee?

Restocking fees should be disclosed in the purchase terms. Fees that were not disclosed or that exceed the disclosed amount can be disputed with the merchant.

What is a home warranty service call fee?

A service call or trip fee is charged each time a technician visits. Per-appliance fees apply if multiple appliances are serviced during the same visit under some warranty plans.

What are common renovation contract pitfalls?

Double-counted overhead where profit is applied to costs that already include markup, change order pricing above base contract rates, and permit fee padding above actual government costs are areas to review.

Common Misconceptions

"The advertised price is the total cost." Many services add fees, surcharges, and taxes that are not included in the advertised price. The final cost may be significantly higher than the initial quote.

"I can cancel at any time without penalty." Many contracts have minimum terms, cancellation fees, or notice periods that make it costly or difficult to cancel before the end of the term.

"My insurance or warranty covers everything." Insurance policies and warranty contracts typically have exclusions, deductibles, and coverage limits. Understanding what is not covered is as important as understanding what is covered.

"All providers charge similar prices." Pricing can vary significantly between providers for the same service. Shopping around and comparing quotes is recommended.
