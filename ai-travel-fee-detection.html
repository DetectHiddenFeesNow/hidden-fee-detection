Frequently Asked Questions About Travel Fees

What are resort fees and why do hotels charge them?

Resort fees are mandatory daily charges for hotel amenities like pools, gyms, and Wi-Fi. They range from $15 to $50 per night and are not included in advertised room rates. AI analysis can identify resort fees on your hotel bill.

Do airlines charge for carry-on bags?

Some airlines charge $25 to $35 for carry-on bags on basic economy fares. Most standard fares include carry-on bags at no additional cost. Checked bags cost $30 to $40 each way for the first bag.

Can rental car insurance be duplicated?

Yes. Rental car collision damage waiver fees of $15 to $30 per day may duplicate coverage from your personal auto insurance policy or premium credit card. AI analysis can identify this potential duplication.

What are typical vacation rental cleaning fees?

Vacation rental cleaning fees range from $50 to $300 per stay depending on the property size and location. These fees are charged on top of the nightly rate and are non-refundable in most cases.

How can I avoid airline change fees?

Choose refundable or main cabin fares that allow changes without fees. Basic economy fares typically prohibit changes. Some airlines offer free changes on certain fare types.

What is a rental car fuel surcharge?

Rental car companies charge $8 to $12 per gallon if you return the car with less than a full tank. Prepaid fuel options allow you to purchase a full tank at a discounted rate upfront.

Are hotel parking fees negotiable?

Some hotels will waive parking fees for loyalty program members or guests who booked directly. Parking fees in urban areas typically range from $20 to $60 per night.

Can AI help me get refunds for travel fees?

AI analysis identifies specific fees on your travel documents and provides information about each fee type that can be used when requesting refunds or credits from travel providers.

What documents can AI analyze for travel fees?

Hotel bills, airline receipts, rental car agreements, vacation rental invoices, cruise invoices, and travel insurance policies can all be analyzed for hidden fees.

How long does AI travel fee analysis take?
