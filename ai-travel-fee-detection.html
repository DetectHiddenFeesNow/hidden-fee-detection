Hotel Fee Breakdown

Resort Fees

Resort fees of $15-$50/night are mandatory charges for amenities guests may not use. AI identifies resort fees on hotel bills.

Parking Fees

Parking fees of $20-$60/night apply in urban and resort areas. AI identifies parking fees.

Wi-Fi Fees

Wi-Fi fees of $10-$20/day apply at many hotels. AI identifies internet fees.

Pet Fees

Pet fees of $25-$150/stay apply at pet-friendly hotels. AI identifies pet fees.

Cancellation Penalties

Cancellation policies range from free cancellation to full charge depending on rate type. AI identifies cancellation terms.

Airline Fee Breakdown

Baggage Fees

Checked bag fees of $30-$40 each way. Overweight fees of $50-$200. AI identifies baggage fees on receipts.

Seat Selection Fees

Seat selection fees of $10-$100 for preferred seats and extra legroom. AI identifies seat charges.

Change and Cancellation Fees

Change fees of $0-$200 per ticket depending on fare type. AI identifies change policies.

Rental Car Fee Breakdown

Insurance Markups

Collision damage waiver of $15-$30/day may duplicate existing coverage from personal auto insurance or credit cards. AI identifies insurance charges.

Fuel Charges

Refueling fees of $8-$12/gallon if returned with less than full tank. AI identifies fuel charges.

Additional Driver Fees

Additional driver fees of $10-$15/day per driver. AI identifies extra driver charges.

Vacation Rental Fee Breakdown

Cleaning Fees

Cleaning fees of $50-$300 per stay are charged in addition to the nightly rate. These fees cover professional cleaning between guests and are disclosed separately from the nightly rate. AI identifies cleaning fees on rental invoices.

Service Fees

Booking platform service fees of 5 to 15 percent of the total booking amount apply to most vacation rental bookings. These fees cover platform costs including payment processing, customer support, and booking services. AI identifies service fees.

Damage Waivers

Damage waiver fees of $10 to $50 per stay cover accidental damage to the property up to certain limits. Exclusions apply for intentional damage or specific types of loss. AI identifies damage waiver charges.

Occupancy Taxes

Transient occupancy taxes of 5 to 15 percent apply to vacation rentals in most jurisdictions. These taxes are not always included in the advertised nightly rate and are added at checkout. AI identifies occupancy tax charges.

How to Avoid Travel Hidden Fees

Compare total cost including all fees when booking hotels and vacation rentals. Use credit cards with no foreign transaction fees for international travel. Pack carry-on only to avoid checked bag fees. Decline rental car insurance if your personal auto policy or credit card provides coverage. Book directly with hotels that include Wi-Fi and parking in their rates. Read cancellation policies before booking non-refundable fare types. Join hotel and airline loyalty programs for fee waivers and complimentary benefits.
