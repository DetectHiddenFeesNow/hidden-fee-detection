What AI Moving Fee Detection Identifies

Weight Re-Weigh Manipulation

Interstate moving companies charge based on weight. AI detects discrepancies between the certified scale weight and the mover's scale weight exceeding the industry tolerance of 100 pounds, re-weigh fees that exceed the actual cost of re-weighing, and inflated weight tickets that include the weight of the moving truck itself.

Stair and Elevator Fee Overcharges

Moving companies add fees for flights of stairs. AI detects stair fees charged when the move was on the ground floor, fees for more flights of stairs than actually exist at the property, and elevator fees charged at buildings with no elevator access.

Long Carry Surcharge Fraud

Long carry fees apply when movers must walk more than a certain distance from the truck to the door. AI detects long carry charges when the distance from the truck to the door is within the standard included distance, fees calculated at rates exceeding the contract rate per foot, and multiple long carry charges for a single property.

Packing Material Markups

Moving companies sell packing supplies at substantial markups. AI detects packing tape at $5 to $10 per roll when retail is $2 to $3, boxes at $3 to $8 each when retail is $1 to $3, and bubble wrap at inflated rates. Markups of 300 to 500 percent above retail are common.

Deposit and Advance Payment Retention

Moving companies require deposits that are supposed to be refundable. AI detects deposits retained for moves that were canceled within the allowed cancellation window, deposits applied to charges that were not disclosed in the original estimate, and advance payments not credited against the final invoice total.

Fuel Surcharge Manipulation

Fuel surcharges are calculated based on the Department of Energy fuel index. AI detects fuel surcharge percentages that exceed the DOE index, surcharges calculated on the total including other surcharges rather than the base rate, and fuel surcharges on local moves where the index typically does not apply.

Piano and Specialty Item Fees

Moving specialty items like pianos, pool tables, and safes requires additional fees. AI detects specialty fees for items that do not require special handling, fees that exceed the disclosed amount in the estimate, and multiple specialty fees for a single item.

Common Misconceptions

"The advertised price is the total cost." Many services add fees, surcharges, and taxes that are not included in the advertised price. The final cost may be significantly higher than the initial quote.

"I can cancel at any time without penalty." Many contracts have minimum terms, cancellation fees, or notice periods that make it costly or difficult to cancel before the end of the term.

"My insurance or warranty covers everything." Insurance policies and warranty contracts typically have exclusions, deductibles, and coverage limits. Understanding what is not covered is as important as understanding what is covered.

"All providers charge similar prices." Pricing can vary significantly between providers for the same service. Shopping around and comparing quotes is recommended.

Red Flags

  • Fees that are not clearly described or are listed in vague terms
  • Charges that seem disproportionate to the service provided
  • Auto-renewal clauses with short cancellation windows
  • Contract terms that are significantly longer than industry standard
  • Pressure to sign or pay without time to review the full agreement
  • Mandatory arbitration clauses that limit legal recourse
