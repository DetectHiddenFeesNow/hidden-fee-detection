What AI Subscription Fee Detection Identifies

Shipping Fee Overcharges

Subscription boxes include shipping in the price or charge separately. AI detects shipping fees that exceed actual delivery costs by 100 to 200 percent, shipping upgrades automatically applied without consent, and multiple shipping fees when items ship from the same warehouse in separate boxes.

Billing Frequency Manipulation

Monthly subscriptions should bill every 30 to 31 days. AI detects billing cycles of 28 days that result in 13 charges per year instead of 12, quarterly plans that bill every 85 days instead of every 91, and annual plans that automatically renew at higher monthly rates.

Curation Fee Inflation

Personalization or curation fees are added to box prices. AI detects curation fees that are described as included in the box price but appear as separate charges on billing statements, tiers where the curation fee increases without a corresponding increase in box value, and skip-a-month fees for pausing delivery.

Cancelation Traps

Subscription cancelation requires notice before a specific date. AI detects cancelation windows of 5 to 10 days before the billing date that are unreasonably short, cancelation processes that require phone calls during limited hours, and fees for canceling that are not disclosed in the original terms.
