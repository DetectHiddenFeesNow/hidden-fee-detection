What AI Car Rental Fee Detection Identifies

Collision Damage Waiver Upsells

CDW waives the renter's liability for damage to the rental vehicle, costing $15 to $35 per day. AI detects CDW coverage that duplicates the renter's existing auto insurance or credit card coverage, CDW accepted at the counter after the renter declined online but the charge still appears, and CDW daily rates that exceed the maximum stated in the rental agreement.

Fuel Service Overcharges

Prepaid fuel options charge for a full tank at above-market rates. AI detects fuel charges applied when the customer returned the vehicle with the same fuel level as pickup, prepaid fuel at rates exceeding local gas station prices by 50 to 100 percent, and fuel surcharges on electric or hybrid vehicles that do not use gasoline.

Toll Fee Processing Markups

Rental companies offer electronic toll passes that include daily access fees plus toll charges. AI detects daily access fees that exceed disclosed amounts, toll processing administrative fees that are higher than the toll operator charges, and toll charges for routes that the vehicle did not travel based on GPS audit log analysis.

Mileage Overage Errors

Rental agreements specify mileage limits and per-mile overage charges. AI detects mileage overage charges based on incorrect starting mileage, per-mile rates that exceed the amount stated in the rental agreement, and multiple mileage overage charges for a single trip when the system failed to reset.

Damage Claim Inflation

Rental companies issue damage claims for vehicle repairs after the vehicle is returned. AI detects administrative fees that exceed the actual processing cost, loss of use charges for days when the vehicle was available for rental, diminished value claims that are not authorized by the rental agreement, and repair estimates that include upgrades or improvements rather than like-kind repairs.

Additional Driver Fee Overcharges

Adding a second driver to a rental agreement incurs a daily fee. AI detects additional driver fees for spouses when the rental company policy waives this fee for spouses, additional driver fees for business rentals where the company account covers all authorized drivers, and fees that exceed the amount disclosed at reservation.

Young Renter and Senior Driver Surcharges

Renters under 25 or over 70 often face daily surcharges. AI detects young renter surcharges applied to renters who are 25 or older, senior surcharges applied based on age limits that differ from the published policy, and surcharges that exceed the maximum amount disclosed in the rental agreement.

Airport and Location Surcharges

Rental counters at airports add customer facility charges, airport concession fees, and vehicle license recovery fees. AI detects facility charges that exceed the approved amount for that specific airport, concession recovery fees that are calculated on the pre-discount rate rather than the actual rental rate, and location surcharges that apply to off-airport locations incorrectly.

Common Misconceptions

"The advertised price is the total cost." Many services add fees, surcharges, and taxes that are not included in the advertised price. The final cost may be significantly higher than the initial quote.

"I can cancel at any time without penalty." Many contracts have minimum terms, cancellation fees, or notice periods that make it costly or difficult to cancel before the end of the term.

"My insurance or warranty covers everything." Insurance policies and warranty contracts typically have exclusions, deductibles, and coverage limits. Understanding what is not covered is as important as understanding what is covered.

"All providers charge similar prices." Pricing can vary significantly between providers for the same service. Shopping around and comparing quotes is recommended.
