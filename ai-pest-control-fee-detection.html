What AI Pest Control Fee Detection Identifies

Chemical Markups

Pest control chemicals are relatively inexpensive at wholesale. AI detects chemical application charges at 300 to 600 percent above wholesale cost, general-use pesticides charged at specialty product rates, and products billed by volume that exceed the amount actually needed for the property size.

Treatment Frequency Inflation

Quarterly treatments are standard for most pests. AI detects monthly treatment schedules that are unnecessary for the specific pest and region, add-on services for pests not present that are bundled into every visit, and warranty call fees charged for re-treatments between scheduled visits when the warranty should cover them free.

Auto-Renewal Traps

Pest control contracts auto-renew at higher rates. AI detects renewal pricing that exceeds the initial contract rate by 20 to 50 percent without disclosure, cancellation fees that increase over time, and notice periods of 60 to 90 days that force customers to pay for months of service they cannot cancel.
