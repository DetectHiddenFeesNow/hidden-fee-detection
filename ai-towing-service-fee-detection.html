What AI Towing Fee Detection Identifies

Hook-Up Fee Double Charging

The hook-up fee covers attaching the vehicle to the tow truck. AI detects hook-up fees of $100-$200 that duplicate the base tow charge, multiple hook-up fees for a single tow, and hook-up fees that are charged even when winching is not required.

Mileage Overcharges

Towing rates include a per-mile charge after a base distance. AI detects mileage calculated from the tow company's starting location rather than the vehicle's location, mileage rates that exceed the maximum rate filed with local authorities, and mileage for distances not actually traveled.

Storage Fee Inflation

Storage fees accumulate daily. AI detects storage fees that exceed the maximum daily rate set by city or county ordinance, gate fees for accessing the lot during business hours, and administrative fees for lien processing that are not authorized by state law.

After-Hours Surcharge Misapplication

After-hours towing costs more. AI detects after-hours surcharges applied during normal business hours, weekend surcharges applied on days other than Saturday and Sunday, and holiday surcharges applied on days that are not federally recognized holidays.
