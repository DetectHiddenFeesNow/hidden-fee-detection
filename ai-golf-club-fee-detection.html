Monthly Minimum Traps

Dining and pro shop minimums require monthly spending. AI detects monthly minimums of $50 to $200 that must be spent regardless of usage, unused minimum amounts that are charged as a penalty but labeled differently, and minimums that increase annually without a cap.

Cart Fee Inflation

Golf cart fees apply per round. AI detects mandatory cart fees for walking members who prefer to walk, cart fees that exceed the club's cost of providing carts by 3 to 5 times, and trail fees for personal carts that are equal to or higher than rental cart pricing.

Special Assessment Padding

Capital assessments fund major improvements. AI detects special assessments for projects that should be covered by regular dues, assessments that are voted on without proper membership approval, and assessment amounts that exceed the actual cost of the project being funded.
