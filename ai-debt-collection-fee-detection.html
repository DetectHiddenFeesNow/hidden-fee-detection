What AI Debt Collection Fee Detection Identifies

Illegal Collection Fees

Collection agencies charge fees that are capped by state law. AI detects collection fees that exceed the state statutory limit of 25 to 33 percent of the original debt, fees calculated on an inflated principal amount that includes other illegal fees, and collection fees on debts where the original contract prohibits collection fees beyond court costs.

Interest Rate Manipulation

Interest rates on collection accounts are governed by the original contract and state law. AI detects interest rates that exceed the contract maximum, interest applied to principal that includes illegal fees creating compound overcharges, and post-judgment interest rates that exceed the statutory rate.

Principal Padding

Collection agencies add their own fees to the original debt. AI detects principal amounts that exceed the original debt plus allowed interest, fees added before the agency had legal right to collect, and multiple agencies adding fees to the same debt as it is sold between collectors.

Statute of Limitation Violations

Old debts may be uncollectible. AI detects collection attempts on debts where the state statute of limitations has expired, lawsuit threats for time-barred debts which is illegal under the FDCPA, and partial payment demands that would restart the statute of limitations without proper disclosure.

Credit Report Errors

Collection accounts on credit reports contain numerous errors. AI detects collection accounts reporting after the 7-year reporting period has expired, incorrect balance amounts that exceed the actual debt, and duplicate collection entries for the same debt sold to multiple agencies.

FAQ

What is the most common hidden fee in pest control contracts?

Chemical markups and treatment frequency inflation where monthly treatments are scheduled when quarterly treatments would be standard for the pest type and region.

Can AI detect HVAC equipment markups?

Yes. AI can analyze HVAC installation contracts to identify equipment pricing above wholesale markups, warranty duplication, and refrigerant overcharges.

What are storage unit rate increase limits?

Storage contracts specify rate increase terms that vary by facility. Some contracts cap increases at a specific percentage, while others do not have caps.

How can I challenge a furniture restocking fee?

Restocking fees should be disclosed in the purchase terms. Fees that were not disclosed or that exceed the disclosed amount can be disputed with the merchant.

What is a home warranty service call fee?

A service call or trip fee is charged each time a technician visits. Per-appliance fees apply if multiple appliances are serviced during the same visit under some warranty plans.

What are common renovation contract pitfalls?

Double-counted overhead where profit is applied to costs that already include markup, change order pricing above base contract rates, and permit fee padding above actual government costs are areas to review.
