Common Hidden Fees in Construction Contracts

Change Order Inflation

Change orders modify the original contract scope. Contractors may charge premium rates for change order work, apply different markup percentages, or include minimum change order amounts that make small modifications disproportionately expensive.

Material Markups

General contractors typically add a markup to material costs. The markup percentage should be specified in the contract. Markups applied to the total material cost including the contractor's own discounts can result in double-counting.

Progress Payment Manipulation

Construction contracts typically specify a payment schedule tied to project milestones. Schedule manipulation can result in payments being made before the corresponding work is completed, reducing the owner's leverage if issues arise.
