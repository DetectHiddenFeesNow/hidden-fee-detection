Common Hidden Fees in Government Services

Third-Party Service Provider Markups

Many government services can be accessed directly through the agency at a lower cost than through third-party service providers. Passport expediting services, visa processing companies, property tax appeal services, and birth certificate ordering services all charge fees above the government's base fee. While some third-party services provide convenience, the markup can sometimes be significant relative to the actual government fee.

Convenience and Processing Fees

Government agencies often contract with third-party payment processors that charge convenience fees for online or phone payments. These fees may be a flat amount per transaction or a percentage of the payment amount. Some processing fees may exceed the actual cost of payment processing, particularly for smaller transactions.

Expedited and Rush Fees

Many government services offer expedited processing for an additional fee. Passport processing, visa applications, permit reviews, and license renewals all have standard and expedited fee structures. The value of expedited processing depends on whether the faster timeline is actually needed for the applicant's situation.
