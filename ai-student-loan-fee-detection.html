What AI Student Loan Fee Detection Identifies

Origination Fee Overcharges

Federal student loans have statutory origination fees set by Congress. For 2025-2026, Direct Subsidized and Unsubsidized loans have an origination fee of 1.057 percent while Direct PLUS loans have a 4.228 percent fee. AI detection identifies when these statutory percentages are exceeded, when fees are calculated on the wrong loan amount, and when private student loan origination fees exceed what was disclosed in the loan offer. Private loan origination fees vary widely from 0 to 5 percent, and AI cross-references each fee against the specific lender's disclosed rates to identify discrepancies.

Interest Capitalization Errors

Interest capitalization occurs when unpaid interest is added to the principal balance, increasing the total cost of the loan. AI detects capitalization events that occur at times not permitted by the loan agreement, capitalization of interest that should have been covered by subsidies, and multiple capitalizations within periods where only one is permitted. A single improper capitalization event can add thousands of dollars to the total loan cost over its lifetime.

Deferment and Forbearance Misapplication

Loan servicers process deferment and forbearance requests, but errors are common. AI detects forbearances applied when the borrower qualified for an income-driven repayment plan instead, fees assessed during periods when interest should have been subsidized, and servicers placing loans in forbearance without borrower consent. The Department of Education has fined multiple servicers for improper forbearance steering, affecting hundreds of thousands of borrowers.

Income-Driven Repayment Calculation Errors

Income-driven repayment plans calculate monthly payments based on discretionary income. AI detects calculation errors including incorrect family size application, use of adjusted gross income when the borrower filed taxes separately, failure to recertify income annually as required, and miscalculation of the poverty line percentage used in the formula. These errors can result in monthly payments that are 20 to 50 percent higher than legally required.

Public Service Loan Forgiveness Tracking Errors

PSLF requires 120 qualifying payments while working for a qualifying employer. AI detects payment counting errors where servicers fail to credit eligible payments, incorrect employer certification processing, and denial of forgiveness based on incorrect payment counts. The PSLF program has a historically high denial rate, with many denials stemming from servicer tracking errors rather than actual borrower ineligibility.

Interest Rate Miscalculations

Student loans have specific interest rate formulas based on loan type and disbursement date. AI detects when variable rate adjustments exceed contractual maximums, when interest accrues at the wrong rate after consolidation, and when promotional rates are not applied as promised. Private student loan variable rates are particularly prone to calculation errors because they track complex index-based formulas.

Late Fee Overcharges

Student loan late fees are regulated by federal law for federal loans and by state usury laws for private loans. AI detects late fees that exceed regulatory maximums, late fees assessed when payments were made within grace periods, and multiple late fees for a single missed payment. Federal student loan late fees are capped at 6 cents per dollar of the overdue payment.

Consolidation Loan Fee Errors

Federal direct consolidation loans combine multiple student loans into a single loan. AI detects consolidation fees that exceed statutory limits, weighted interest rate calculations that are incorrectly computed, and consolidation of loans that should have been kept separate to preserve borrower benefits. The weighted average interest rate formula is prone to rounding errors that can increase total interest costs.

Types of Student Loan Documents Reviewed

Master Promissory Notes

The MPN is the binding agreement between the borrower and the lender. AI reviews the MPN for origination fee disclosures, interest rate terms, repayment options, deferment and forbearance rights, and prepayment penalties. Discrepancies between the MPN terms and actual loan servicing are flagged for investigation.

Loan Disclosure Statements

Disclosure statements itemize the loan amount, fees, interest rate, and disbursement schedule. AI cross-references each disclosure against the MPN terms and identifies origination fees that exceed disclosed amounts, disbursement timing errors, and loan amounts that do not match the certified cost of attendance.

Billing Statements

Monthly billing statements show payment due dates, amounts due, interest accrual, and principal balances. AI detects incorrect payment allocation, interest capitalization events that are not disclosed, late fees that exceed regulatory limits, and payment posting delays that result in improper late fee assessments.

Income-Driven Repayment Plan Applications

IDR applications determine monthly payments based on income and family size. AI detects errors in payment calculation, recertification timing, and plan eligibility. The system also flags cases where borrowers on IDR plans are incorrectly placed into standard repayment or forbearance.

Loan Consolidation Applications

Consolidation applications include loan listings, interest rate calculations, and repayment term selections. AI detects consolidation fees, weighted average interest rate miscalculations, and loss of borrower benefits such as interest rate discounts or principal rebates that occur when certain loans are consolidated.

Default and Rehabilitation Agreements

Default rehabilitation agreements set terms for bringing defaulted loans back to good standing. AI detects rehabilitation fees that exceed allowable amounts, collection cost charges that are improperly calculated, and income information that is incorrectly used in rehabilitation payment calculations.

Real-World Fee Detection Examples

Case Study: Improper Interest Capitalization on Federal Loans

A borrower with multiple federal student loans requested deferment during graduate school. Upon entering repayment, the servicer capitalized all unpaid interest from all loans simultaneously. The Master Promissory Note permitted capitalization only at specific events such as entering repayment after a grace period. AI analysis revealed that interest was capitalized twice during a 12-month period when the note only permitted once. The improper capitalization added $1,240 to the borrower's principal balance.

Case Study: Income-Driven Repayment Miscalculation

A borrower with a family of four filed taxes as married filing separately. The servicer used the combined household income instead of the individual income, resulting in a monthly payment of $680 instead of the correct amount of $315. This error persisted for 14 months before AI detection identified the discrepancy. The borrower received a refund of $5,110 in overpayments and a corrected payment going forward.

Case Study: Private Loan Variable Rate Calculation Error

A private student loan borrower had a variable rate loan tied to the LIBOR index plus 4.5 percent. AI analysis revealed that the servicer was applying an incorrect margin of 5.25 percent instead of the contractual 4.5 percent. The error resulted in an overcharge of 0.75 percent on a $45,000 balance for 18 months, totaling $506 in excess interest charges.
