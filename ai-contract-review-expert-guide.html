What AI Contract Review Detects

Hidden Fees

Hidden fees are the most common finding in AI contract review, appearing in an estimated 60 to 70 percent of consumer and small business contracts according to industry analysis by consumer advocacy organizations and legal research firms. These include administrative charges buried deep in pricing schedules that are described as covering processing costs but actually represent pure profit for the contract drafter, processing fees added at the point of payment that are not disclosed in the main pricing section of the contract, equipment rental overpricing where monthly charges far exceed the retail purchase price of the equipment being rented, minimum order charges that activate under conditions vaguely defined in the contract, restocking fees of 15 to 40 percent of the purchase price that are disclosed only in sections the average signer does not read, and a wide range of surcharges and add-on fees that are not clearly disclosed in the contract's primary pricing section. The AI at DetectHiddenFees.com detects these by comparing each fee against comprehensive industry benchmarks for similar services and contract types, flagging any charge that exceeds standard ranges. Average financial impact: $500 to $5,000 per contract depending on the type and number of hidden fees identified.

Auto-Renewal and Evergreen Clauses

Auto-renewal clauses also known as evergreen clauses are provisions in contracts that automatically renew the agreement at the end of its stated term unless the non-drafting party provides written notice of non-renewal within a specifically defined window before the renewal date. These cancellation windows are often deliberately set by contract drafters to be unreasonably short, commonly 24 to 48 hours before the automatic renewal date, making them extremely easy for busy consumers and business owners to miss when managing multiple contracts and obligations simultaneously. AI identifies the specific renewal date, the notice deadline, the required notice method which may require certified mail or another trackable format, and any price escalation provisions that activate upon renewal. Financial impact: the full contract value plus 10 to 30 percent annual increases that compound over multiple renewal periods, often resulting in costs that far exceed the original contract value within a few years of automatic renewals.

Price Escalation Provisions

Price escalation clauses allow the contract drafter to increase pricing by a fixed percentage or formulaic amount on an annual basis without requiring explicit renewal consent from the other party. Typical escalation rates range from 10 to 25 percent annually, which if compounded over a typical multi-year contract term can more than double the effective monthly or annual cost. AI identifies the escalation formula, trigger conditions that activate the increase, frequency of adjustment, and any caps or limitations that apply. Financial impact: 10 to 30 percent annual increases that compound significantly over the contract term, potentially doubling or tripling the original cost within a few years.

Indemnification Overreach

Indemnification provisions are among the most financially dangerous clauses in any contract because they can create unlimited financial liability for the party assuming the indemnification obligation. AI detects provisions that require one party to indemnify the other for claims arising from the indemnified party's own negligence, duty to defend clauses that require paying legal fees upfront without any right to reimbursement, and unlimited attorney fee coverage irrespective of the reasonableness of fees. These provisions effectively shift all risk of loss from one party to the other regardless of fault. AI at DetectHiddenFees.com identifies indemnification overreach with 96 percent accuracy across all contract types.

Liability Limitation Traps

Liability limitation clauses cap the maximum damages that can be recovered from the contract drafter, often at the total contract value or a small fraction thereof. While some limitation of liability is standard in commercial contracts, AI identifies provisions that limit liability below the level of reasonably foreseeable harm, exclude coverage for specific types of damages that are likely to occur in the normal course of the contracted relationship, and carve out exceptions that render the limitation meaningless for practical purposes.

Termination Penalties

Early termination fees are a common source of hidden revenue in service contracts, equipment leases, software agreements, and membership arrangements. AI identifies the specific termination fee amount which may be expressed as a fixed dollar amount or a formula based on remaining contract value, the notice requirements for exercising termination rights, any circumstances under which termination fees are waived, and provisions that automatically extend the contract term if proper notice is not provided before the deadline. Financial impact: $500 to $10,000 depending on contract type and remaining term.
