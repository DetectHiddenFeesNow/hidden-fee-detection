Insurance Fee Categories

Auto Insurance

Auto insurance policies may have premium increases exceeding state-approved rate filings, duplicate coverage, unauthorized add-ons, and administrative fees. Rate increase patterns that exceed normal market adjustments affect policyholders who have been with the same insurer for extended periods.

Health Insurance

Health insurance involves complex billing with out-of-network rules, deductible calculations, and claim processing. Issues include incorrect deductible application, out-of-network billing errors, and pre-existing condition exclusion overreach. Health insurance claim denials based on incorrect coding or coverage determinations are among the most common consumer complaints reported to state insurance departments.

Homeowners Insurance

Homeowners policies may have replacement cost overvaluation that inflates premiums, wind and hail deductible misapplication, and inflation guard endorsements exceeding actual construction cost increases.

Travel Insurance

Travel insurance policies contain pre-existing condition lookback periods, cancel for any reason payout manipulation, and claim denial errors. CFAR coverage advertised at 75 percent reimbursement may pay less after deductibles and fees are applied.

Pet Insurance

Pet insurance claim denials based on incorrect waiting period application and pre-existing condition exclusions are common. The average claim error may result in hundreds of dollars in unpaid benefits.

Life Insurance

Life insurance policies have cost of insurance adjustments, surrender charges, and premium load fees. Policy illustrations should be compared against actual billing to detect cost of insurance increases exceeding contractual maximums.

Common Misconceptions About Insurance

"My insurance covers everything up to the policy limit." Insurance policies have exclusions, deductibles, coinsurance, and coverage limits that apply before the policy limit is reached. Understanding the specific terms of each coverage section is important.

"I can't change my insurance until the renewal date." Many policies can be canceled mid-term, though cancellation fees or short-rate penalties may apply. Shopping for new coverage can often result in savings even before renewal.

"All insurance companies charge similar rates for the same coverage." Premiums for identical coverage can vary significantly between insurers because each company uses its own underwriting criteria and risk assessment models.

Can AI detect errors in insurance claim payments?

Yes. AI can compare claim payment calculations against policy terms to identify potential discrepancies in how deductibles, coverage limits, and exclusions were applied.

How often should I review my insurance policies?

Reviewing policies annually before renewal is recommended. Any major life event such as marriage, home purchase, or new vehicle is also a good time to review coverage.

What is the difference between actual cash value and replacement cost?

Actual cash value pays the depreciated value of damaged property, while replacement cost pays the cost to replace it at current prices. Policies using ACV typically result in lower claim payments.
