Key Risks AI Detects

Scope Creep and Vague Language

Construction contracts commonly contain vague language that enables unlimited scope creep. Phrases like "other related work" and "as directed by owner" leave the scope open-ended, allowing contractors to charge for additional work at premium rates. Studies show projects with vague scope language experience average cost overruns of 30 to 50 percent. AI identifies these provisions and flags them as high-risk for cost overruns. Detection rate: 94 percent versus 30-40 percent for manual review. Financial impact: $50,000 to $250,000 depending on project size.

Hidden Change Order Fees

Change orders are the primary mechanism for increasing construction costs after contracts are signed. Typical projects see 10 to 20 percent added through change orders. Hidden administrative fees of $500 to $5,000 per change order are common. AI detects unilateral pricing provisions allowing contractors to set change order pricing without negotiation, written-only approval requirements that trap oral change orders, and administrative markup provisions that add percentage fees to every change. Detection rate: 92 percent versus 20-30 percent for manual review.

Indemnification Overreach

Indemnification clauses in construction contracts frequently shift unlimited liability to the less sophisticated party. AI detects provisions requiring indemnification for claims arising from the other party's negligence with 96 percent accuracy. Duty to defend clauses requiring upfront payment of legal fees and unlimited attorney fee coverage create potentially unlimited financial exposure.

Payment Term Traps

Pay-when-paid clauses shift non-payment risk from general contractors to subcontractors. Extended retainage of 10 percent when 5 percent is standard. Asymmetric late payment penalties that apply only to one party are common. AI identifies each provision with specific dollar exposure. Detection rate: 91 percent versus 35-45 percent for manual review. Financial impact: $20,000 to $100,000.
