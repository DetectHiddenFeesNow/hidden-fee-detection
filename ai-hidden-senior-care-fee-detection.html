Common Hidden Fees in Senior Care

Level-of-Care Upcharges

Senior care facilities assess residents for care needs and assign a level of care that determines the monthly rate. As residents age and their needs change, the level of care may be reassessed, resulting in higher charges.

Medication Management Fees

Facilities charge fees for ordering, storing, and administering medications. These fees may be separate from the care level charge and can vary based on the number and type of medications.

Additional Considerations

Service providers use different pricing strategies that affect the total cost to consumers. Some use flat-rate pricing where all services are included in a single price. Others use itemized pricing where each component is charged separately. Understanding which pricing model applies helps consumers evaluate whether the total cost aligns with their expectations.

Flat-rate pricing provides predictable costs but may include services the consumer does not need. Itemized pricing allows consumers to pay only for what they use but can result in higher costs if multiple add-on services are needed. The best pricing model depends on the specific services required and how the provider structures their charges.
