What AI Contract Review Detects

Hidden Fees

Hidden fees are the most common finding in AI contract review, appearing in an estimated 60 to 70 percent of consumer and small business contracts. These include administrative charges buried in pricing schedules, processing fees described as covering costs but representing pure profit, equipment rental overpricing, minimum order charges, restocking fees, and a wide range of surcharges that are not clearly disclosed in the contract's primary pricing terms. The AI at DetectHiddenFees.com detects these by comparing each fee against industry benchmarks for similar services. Financial impact: $500 to $5,000 per contract depending on the type and number of hidden fees.

Auto-Renewal and Evergreen Clauses

Auto-renewal clauses, also known as evergreen clauses, automatically renew contracts at the end of their term unless the non-drafting party provides notice of non-renewal within a specified window. These cancellation windows are often as short as 24 to 48 hours before the renewal date, making them easy to miss. The AI identifies the specific renewal date, notice deadline, notice method requirements, and any escalation provisions that increase pricing upon renewal. Financial impact: contract value plus 10 to 30 percent annual increases, compounding over multiple renewal periods.

Price Escalation Provisions

Price escalation clauses allow the contract drafter to increase pricing by a fixed percentage or formulaic amount on an annual basis without requiring explicit consent from the other party. These provisions are common in service agreements, software licenses, maintenance contracts, and long-term supply agreements. Typical escalation rates range from 10 to 25 percent annually, which can more than double the contract cost over a five-year term. AI identifies the escalation formula, trigger conditions, frequency of adjustment, and any caps or limitations on increases.

Indemnification Overreach

Indemnification provisions are among the most dangerous clauses in any contract because they can create unlimited financial liability. AI detects provisions that require one party to indemnify the other for claims arising from the indemnified party's own negligence, duty to defend clauses that require paying legal fees upfront without reimbursement, and unlimited attorney fee coverage. These provisions effectively shift all risk of loss to the less sophisticated party. AI identifies indemnification overreach with 96 percent accuracy across all contract types.

Liability Limitation Traps

Liability limitation clauses cap the maximum damages that can be recovered from the contract drafter, often at the total contract value or a fraction thereof. While some limitation is standard, AI identifies provisions that limit liability below the level of potential harm, exclude coverage for specific types of damages that are likely to occur, and carve out exceptions that render the limitation meaningless. The AI compares the limitation language against industry standards and the specific risk profile of the contracted services.

Arbitration and Venue Requirements

Mandatory arbitration clauses require disputes to be resolved through private arbitration rather than through the court system, often waiving the right to a jury trial. AI identifies arbitration requirements, analyzes the cost allocation provisions that determine who pays arbitration fees, evaluates venue selection clauses that may require dispute resolution in unfavorable geographic locations, and flags class action waivers that prevent consumers from joining together in claims. These provisions can significantly increase the cost and difficulty of pursuing legitimate claims.

Termination Penalties

Early termination fees are common in service contracts, equipment leases, software agreements, and membership arrangements. AI identifies the specific termination fee amount, notice requirements for termination, any circumstances under which termination fees are waived, and provisions that automatically extend the contract term if proper notice is not provided. Financial impact: $500 to $10,000 depending on contract type and remaining term.
