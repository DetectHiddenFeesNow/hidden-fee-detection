What AI Contract Review Detects

Hidden Fees

Hidden fees are the most common finding in AI contract review, appearing in an estimated 60 to 70 percent of consumer and small business contracts according to industry analysis. These include administrative charges buried deep in pricing schedules, processing fees described as covering costs but actually representing pure profit for the contract drafter, equipment rental overpricing where monthly charges far exceed the purchase price of the equipment, minimum order charges that activate under conditions not clearly disclosed, restocking fees of 15 to 40 percent of purchase price, and a wide range of surcharges and add-on fees that are not clearly disclosed in the contract's primary pricing section. The AI at DetectHiddenFees.com detects these by comparing each fee against comprehensive industry benchmarks for similar services and contract types. Financial impact: $500 to $5,000 per contract depending on type and number of hidden fees.

Auto-Renewal and Evergreen Clauses

Auto-renewal clauses, also known as evergreen clauses, are provisions that automatically renew contracts at the end of their stated term unless the non-drafting party provides written notice of non-renewal within a specifically defined window before the renewal date. These cancellation windows are often deliberately set to be as short as 24 to 48 hours before the renewal date, making them extremely easy to miss. The AI identifies the specific renewal date, notice deadline, required notice method, and any price escalation provisions that activate upon renewal. Financial impact: contract value plus 10 to 30 percent annual increases compounding over multiple renewal periods, often resulting in total costs that far exceed the original contract value within a few years.

Price Escalation Provisions

Price escalation clauses allow contract drafters to increase pricing by a fixed percentage or formula on an annual basis without requiring explicit consent. Typical escalation rates range from 10 to 25 percent annually, more than doubling contract costs over a 5-year term. AI identifies the escalation formula, trigger conditions, frequency of adjustment, and any caps or limitations. Financial impact: 10 to 30 percent annual increases that compound significantly over the contract term.

Indemnification Overreach

Indemnification provisions can create unlimited financial liability. AI detects provisions requiring indemnification for claims arising from the indemnified party's own negligence, duty to defend clauses requiring upfront legal fee payment, and unlimited attorney fee coverage. AI identifies indemnification overreach with 96 percent accuracy across all contract types.

Liability Limitation Traps

Liability limitation clauses cap maximum recoverable damages. AI identifies limits below the level of potential harm, exclusions that render the limitation meaningless, and carve-outs for specific damage types. The AI compares limitation language against industry standards and the specific risk profile of the contracted services.

Arbitration and Venue Requirements

Mandatory arbitration clauses require dispute resolution through private arbitration rather than court, often waiving jury trial rights. AI identifies arbitration requirements, analyzes cost allocation provisions, evaluates venue selection clauses that may require dispute resolution in unfavorable locations, and flags class action waivers that prevent consumers from joining together in claims against the contract drafter.

Red Flags to Watch For

  • Charges that are not explained or are described in vague terms
  • Fees that appear to duplicate other listed charges
  • Contracts with automatic renewal without adequate cancellation notice
  • Prices that increase after the initial promotional period without notice
  • Service fees that are added to charges that should be included in the base price

Common Misconceptions

"The final bill will match the initial quote." Additional fees may be added for services or items that were not included in the initial quote.

"All charges will be clearly explained." Some charges may appear on invoices or billing statements without clear descriptions.

Step-by-Step Checklist

  1. Review the complete contract or service agreement before signing
  2. Compare the final invoice against the initial quote
  3. Ask for clarification on any charge that is not clearly described
  4. Check for automatic renewal provisions and cancellation terms
  5. Document all communications with the provider
  6. Upload your documents for AI analysis
