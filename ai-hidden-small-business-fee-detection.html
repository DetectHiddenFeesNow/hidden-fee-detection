Small Business Fee Categories

Merchant Services

Credit card processing agreements involve interchange fees, assessment fees, payment gateway charges, monthly minimums, and chargeback fees. Some processors use tiered pricing where qualified, mid-qualified, and non-qualified rates vary significantly. Statement fees, annual fees, and PCI compliance fees may add to the total cost. AI analysis can help highlight fee structures that may result in higher total costs compared to flat-rate or interchange-plus pricing models.

Vendor and Supplier Contracts

Business vendor contracts may contain auto-renewal clauses, price escalation provisions, minimum purchase requirements, and volume discount structures that are not always straightforward. AI analysis can help identify contract terms that may warrant review, including renewal notice periods, price adjustment formulas, and termination provisions.

Software Subscriptions

Business software subscriptions involve per-seat licensing, tier upgrades, auto-renewal terms, and usage-based pricing that can escalate costs.

Business Insurance

Commercial insurance policies have premium calculations, coverage limits, deductibles, and exclusions that differ from personal insurance. Workers compensation premiums are based on payroll classification codes that may be applied incorrectly.
