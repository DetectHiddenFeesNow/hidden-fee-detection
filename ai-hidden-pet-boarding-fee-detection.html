Common Hidden Fees in Pet Boarding

Medication Administration Fees

Pet boarding facilities charge additional fees for administering medications. Fees may be charged per dose or per day, and the total can approach or exceed the cost of boarding itself for pets requiring multiple daily medications. Some facilities charge different rates for oral medications versus injections.

Medication handling fees should be disclosed in the service agreement. Comparing fees between facilities helps identify pricing differences for this service.

Holiday and Peak Period Surcharges

Many boarding facilities apply surcharges during holiday periods and peak travel times. The definition of a holiday period varies between facilities and may extend several days before and after the actual holiday. Surcharges may be a flat daily fee or a percentage above the standard rate.

Common Misconceptions About Pet Boarding Fees

"The daily rate covers everything my pet needs." The daily rate typically covers basic housing and feeding. Additional services like playtime, walks, grooming, and medication administration are charged separately.

"Medication fees are the same at all facilities." Medication administration fees vary significantly between facilities. Comparing these fees separately from the boarding rate helps identify pricing differences.

Red Flags in Pet Boarding Contracts

  • Medication fees that are not specified in the service agreement
  • Holiday surcharges that apply for extended periods around holidays
  • Cancellation penalties that apply regardless of timing
  • Mandatory add-on services that are described as optional in promotions
