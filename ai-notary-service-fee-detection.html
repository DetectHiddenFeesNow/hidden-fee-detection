What AI Notary Fee Detection Identifies

Per-Signature Fee Violations

Most states cap notary fees at $5 to $15 per signature. AI detects per-signature fees that exceed the state statutory maximum, flat fees for document packages that exceed the sum of individual signature caps, and minimum fees that apply even for single signatures effectively exceeding the per-signature cap. The most common violation is a $25 minimum fee in states where the per-signature cap is $10.

Travel Fee Inflation

Mobile notaries charge travel fees for visiting clients. AI detects travel fees that exceed the standard mileage reimbursement rate set by the IRS, travel time billed at rates exceeding the notary's per-signature fee, and minimum travel fees for distances under 5 miles that should be included in the base fee.

Loan Signing Double-Billing

Loan signing agents are paid by the title company or lender for notarizing mortgage documents. AI detects signing agents who charge borrowers additional fees for the same service the lender already paid, signing fees that are not disclosed in the Closing Disclosure, and mobile notary fees on top of the standard signing fee for appointments at the borrower's home.
