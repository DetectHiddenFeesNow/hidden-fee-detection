What Roofing Contractors Typically Charge

Roofing costs vary significantly based on roof size, pitch, materials, and geographic location. A typical asphalt shingle roof replacement ranges from $5,000 to $12,000 for an average single-family home. Material costs typically account for 40-50% of the total, labor for 30-40%, and overhead and profit for 10-20%.
