Home Services Fee Categories

Pest Control

Pest control contracts involve chemical markups, treatment frequency inflation, auto-renewal traps, and inspection fee padding. Quarterly treatments are standard for most pests, but some contracts schedule monthly visits that may not be necessary.

HVAC

HVAC installation contracts have equipment markups above wholesale, warranty manipulation where extended plans duplicate manufacturer coverage, refrigerant pricing at multiples of wholesale cost, and permit fee inflation.

Roofing

Roofing contracts may have material markups of 100-300 percent above wholesale, warranty manipulation, storm chaser pricing inflation, and permit fee padding.

Storage Units

Self-storage contracts involve rate increase caps, administrative fee inflation, insurance markups, and late fee overcharges.

Alarm Systems

Home security contracts have monitoring rate escalation, equipment markup, early termination penalties, and auto-renewal traps.

Furniture

Furniture purchases involve delivery fee inflation, warranty plan manipulation, deferred interest financing traps, and restocking fees.

Common Misconceptions About Home Service Contracts

"Free inspection means no cost." Free inspections often lead to upselling of services that may not be necessary. Reviewing inspection results and getting second opinions helps evaluate recommendations.

"The quoted price is what I will pay." Many home service contracts include add-on charges, service fees, and escalation clauses that can increase the total cost above the quoted base price.

"I can cancel at any time." Home service contracts often have minimum terms, auto-renewal provisions, and cancellation fees that make it costly to cancel before the end of the term.

Red Flags in Home Service Contracts

  • Auto-renewal clauses with cancellation windows of 30 days or less
  • Service fees that are not clearly described or quantified
  • Contract terms longer than one year without month-to-month options
  • Material markups that seem significantly above market prices

What is the most common hidden fee in pest control contracts?

Chemical application markups are among the most common issues. Treatment frequency that exceeds what is necessary for the specific pest and region is another area to review.

Can AI detect hidden fees in HVAC installation contracts?

Yes. AI can analyze HVAC contracts to identify equipment markups, warranty duplication where extended plans overlap manufacturer coverage, and refrigerant pricing that may be significantly above wholesale.

How can I avoid overpaying for a home security system?

Compare monitoring rates across providers, understand whether equipment is purchased or leased, calculate the total cost over the full contract term, and ask about early termination penalties before signing.
