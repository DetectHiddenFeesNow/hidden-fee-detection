Home Services Fee Categories

Pest Control

Pest control contracts involve chemical markups, treatment frequency inflation, auto-renewal traps, and inspection fee padding. Quarterly treatments are standard for most pests, but some contracts schedule monthly visits that may not be necessary.

HVAC

HVAC installation contracts have equipment markups above wholesale, warranty manipulation where extended plans duplicate manufacturer coverage, refrigerant pricing at multiples of wholesale cost, and permit fee inflation.

Roofing

Roofing contracts may have material markups of 100-300 percent above wholesale, warranty manipulation, storm chaser pricing inflation, and permit fee padding.

Storage Units

Self-storage contracts involve rate increase caps, administrative fee inflation, insurance markups, and late fee overcharges.

Alarm Systems

Home security contracts have monitoring rate escalation, equipment markup, early termination penalties, and auto-renewal traps.

Furniture

Furniture purchases involve delivery fee inflation, warranty plan manipulation, deferred interest financing traps, and restocking fees.
