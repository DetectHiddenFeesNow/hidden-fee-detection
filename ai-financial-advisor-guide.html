Why Traditional Financial Analysis Falls Short

Human financial reviewers consistently miss 40 to 60 percent of hidden fees for several fundamental reasons. First, fatigue sets in after reviewing multiple line items across multiple documents, causing attention to decline significantly after the first 30 minutes of review. Second, different providers use different names for identical fee types, making it difficult for even experienced reviewers to identify all variations. Fourth, medical billing uses over 10,000 individual CPT codes that require specialized training to interpret accurately.
