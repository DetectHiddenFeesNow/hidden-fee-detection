What AI Tax Fee Detection Identifies

Per-Form Price Stacking

Tax preparers charge per form or schedule. AI detects per-form pricing where the base fee covers only the 1040 and each additional schedule is $20-$50, Schedule C for self-employed taxpayers priced at premium rates despite being common, and minimum fees for simple returns that are 2 to 3 times the cost of online preparation.

Audit Protection Scams

Audit protection plans promise representation in case of IRS audit. AI detects audit protection fees of $40-$60 when the IRS provides free taxpayer assistance and many preparers include audit support in the base fee, coverage that only applies to full IRS audits not correspondence audits which represent 75 percent of all audits, and deductibles or copays that apply before coverage begins.

State Return Overcharges

State returns are a separate fee. AI detects state return preparation fees that are $40-$80 when the state return is substantially a copy of the federal return, state e-file fees of $10-$20 when many states provide free e-filing, and multiple state returns for part-year residents that are priced as individual state returns rather than as a combined return.

E-File Fee Inflation

E-file fees appear as a separate line item. AI detects e-file fees of $10-$30 when the IRS provides free e-filing through authorized providers, per-state e-file fees that are charged separately for each state filed, and convenience fees for paying preparation fees with a credit card that exceed the actual merchant processing cost.
