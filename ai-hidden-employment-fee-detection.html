Common Hidden Fees in Employment Documents

Wage Deduction Errors - Unauthorized or incorrect deductions from paychecks for benefits, uniforms, or training costs that were not agreed to in writing.

Non-Compete Overreach - Restrictive covenants that are broader than necessary to protect legitimate business interests, potentially limiting future employment options.

Severance Miscalculations - Severance pay calculated on incorrect salary figures or missing required benefit continuation provisions.

Expense Reimbursement Issues - Policies that require employees to bear business expenses without timely or full reimbursement.

Misclassification Risks - Independent contractor classification that shifts employment tax and benefit costs to workers who may legally be employees.

Red Flags in Employment Documents

  • Non-compete clauses covering broad geographic areas or long time periods
  • Wage deduction authorization without specific dollar amounts
  • Severance agreements requiring release of claims before seeing the calculation
  • Expense reimbursement policies with discretionary language
  • Arbitration clauses that limit legal recourse for wage disputes

Common Misconceptions

"Non-compete agreements are always enforceable." Enforceability varies by state. Many states limit non-compete agreements to specific situations and may not enforce agreements that are overly broad.

"Severance pay is required by law." Severance pay is not required by federal law. It is provided at the employer's discretion or as specified in employment contracts or company policy.

"Independent contractors have the same rights as employees." Independent contractors are not covered by many employment laws including minimum wage, overtime, workers compensation, and unemployment insurance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can AI detect errors in severance calculations?

Yes. AI can compare severance pay calculations against the terms of the employment agreement or severance offer to identify potential discrepancies in salary basis, benefit continuation, or payment timing.

What is the most common wage deduction error?

Unauthorized deductions for uniforms, equipment, or training costs that were not agreed to in writing are among the most common wage deduction issues reported to state labor departments.

Are non-compete agreements enforceable in all states?

Non-compete enforcement varies significantly by state. Some states prohibit non-competes entirely while others enforce them if they are reasonable in scope, geography, and duration.

What is the difference between an employee and an independent contractor?

The classification depends on the degree of control the employer has over how the work is performed. Misclassification can result in the worker missing out on benefits and protections while the employer avoids tax obligations.
