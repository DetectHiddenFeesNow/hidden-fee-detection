What AI Renovation Fee Detection Identifies

Double-Counted Overhead and Profit

Contractors add overhead and profit percentages to base costs. AI detects overhead applied to materials that already include the contractor's wholesale markup, profit applied to permit fees when permits are a pass-through cost, and both overhead and profit applied to labor that is billed at an hourly rate already including these markups.

Material Price Inflation

Contractors charge for materials at 3 to 5 times wholesale. AI detects material pricing that exceeds wholesale cost by 200 to 400 percent, material quantities that exceed the actual amount needed for the project scope, and credit for unused materials at the end of the project that are not refunded.

Change Order Inflation

Change orders are priced at premium rates. AI detects change order labor rates that are 50 to 100 percent higher than the base contract rate, material costs in change orders at retail pricing when the contractor receives wholesale pricing, and minimum change order fees that apply even for minor adjustments.

Permit Fee Padding

Renovation permits cost $50 to $500. AI detects permit fees padded 200 to 300 percent above actual city cost, multiple permit fees for a single project, and permit fees charged but never actually obtained from the jurisdiction.

Demolition and Disposal Overcharges

Demolition and debris removal are often marked up. AI detects dumpster rental fees that exceed local rental rates by 2 to 3 times, disposal fees that are charged per-ton at rates above the local landfill's posted fees, and demolition labor that is billed at premium rates despite requiring minimal skill.
