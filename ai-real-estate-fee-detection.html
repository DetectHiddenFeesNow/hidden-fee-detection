What AI Real Estate Fee Detection Identifies

Inflated Loan Origination Fees

Lenders charge origination fees calculated as a percentage of the loan amount, typically 0.5 to 1 percent. AI detection identifies origination fees that exceed industry averages for your loan type and credit profile. Some lenders charge the maximum allowable percentage regardless of borrower qualifications, adding thousands in unnecessary costs. A borrower with excellent credit should not pay the same origination percentage as one with marginal credit. AI cross-references your origination fee against averages for borrowers with similar profiles and flags excess charges above 0.3 percent of the typical rate.

Duplicate and Unnecessary Title Fees

Title companies bundle numerous fees into closing costs that are easy to overlook. AI detects duplicate title search fees, simultaneous lender and owner title policy overcharges, settlement fees that exceed local averages, and recording fees marked up above actual government rates. Title fees vary significantly by region, making it difficult for homebuyers to know what is reasonable. AI maintains a database of average title fees by county and flags any charge that exceeds the 75th percentile.

Discount Point Misrepresentation

Discount points allow borrowers to buy down their interest rate. Each point costs 1 percent of the loan amount and typically reduces the rate by 0.25 percent. AI detection identifies when points are charged but the corresponding rate reduction is smaller than standard, when points are listed on the closing disclosure but not reflected in the actual payment calculation, and when negative points (rebates) are misapplied. Mortgage point fraud affects approximately 5 percent of conventional loans according to industry whistleblower reports.

Private Mortgage Insurance Overcharges

PMI premiums vary by loan-to-value ratio, credit score, and lender. AI detects PMI charges that exceed the expected premium for your specific risk profile, PMI that continues after the loan-to-value ratio drops below 80 percent, and lender-paid mortgage insurance that is billed to the borrower through a higher interest rate. The average PMI overcharge is $480 per year.

Escrow Account Padding

Lenders require escrow accounts for property taxes and insurance and are permitted to hold up to two months of cushion beyond expected payments. AI detects escrow accounts funded with excessive cushions, impound accounts on loans that do not require them, and annual escrow analyses that overestimate tax increases. Escrow overcharges average $320 per year.

Prepaid Interest Miscalculations

Prepaid interest covers the period between closing and the first mortgage payment. AI detects prepaid interest calculated using incorrect daily interest rates, too many days covered, or double-counted days. These errors are particularly common when closings occur late in the month. Prepaid interest miscalculations average $180 per closing.

Real Estate Commission Overcharges

Real estate commissions are negotiable despite standard listing agreements showing 5 to 6 percent. AI detects commission agreements that exceed prevailing rates in your market, dual agency situations where the full commission is charged despite lower workload, and commission splits that are not disclosed in writing. Commission overcharges average $4,200 on a $400,000 home.

Recording Fee Markups

Government recording fees are set by local jurisdictions and are publicly available. AI compares recording fees on your closing disclosure against official county fee schedules to detect markups. Some title companies and settlement agents charge $50 to $200 more than the actual recording cost. These markups are illegal in some states but persist because few buyers verify them.

Appraisal Fee Inflation

Appraisal fees in closing disclosures often exceed the actual cost paid to the appraiser. Lenders and mortgage brokers may add administrative fees on top of the appraisal cost without disclosure. AI detects appraisal fees that exceed typical charges for your property type and location by comparing against published fee schedules from major appraisal management companies.

Rental Application and Lease Fee Detection

Rental applications and lease agreements contain numerous hidden charges. AI detects non-refundable application fees exceeding local limits, administrative fees for lease processing, move-in and move-out inspection charges that duplicate normal maintenance, and early termination fees that exceed statutory limits. The average tenant overpays $240 in undisclosed rental fees annually.

Types of Real Estate Documents Reviewed

Loan Estimate Forms

The Loan Estimate is provided within three business days of a mortgage application. AI reviews every fee listed in the origination charges, services borrower did not shop for, services borrower can shop for, and other costs sections. Common findings include inflated origination charges, unnecessary services, and fees that exceed good faith estimates.

Closing Disclosures

The Closing Disclosure is provided three business days before closing and contains the final itemized costs. AI compares each fee against the original Loan Estimate to identify changes that require lender re-disclosure under TRID rules. Unauthorized fee increases and new charges not present in the Loan Estimate are flagged automatically.

Purchase Agreements

Real estate purchase agreements contain contingency clauses, inspection periods, and fee obligations. AI detects non-standard commission structures, buyer-paid fees that are typically seller responsibilities, and hidden transfer taxes that are not included in standard fee estimates. The entire agreement is analyzed for fee-related language that could result in unexpected costs.

Lease and Rental Agreements

Residential and commercial leases contain numerous fee clauses. AI detects hidden amenity fees, parking charges, pet rent that exceeds disclosed amounts, utility billing arrangements that exceed actual usage, mandatory valet trash fees, and renewal rent increases that exceed contractual caps. Lease fee detection saves tenants an average of $360 per year.

Title Reports and Commitments

Title commitments list encumbrances, easements, and requirements for clearing title. AI detects title insurance rate overcharges, simultaneous issue discounts that are not applied, and endorsements that are required but unnecessary based on property characteristics.

Home Inspection Reports

Home inspection contracts often contain fee escalation clauses, travel charges, and re-inspection fees. AI detects minimum inspection fees that exceed reasonable amounts, mold testing upsells that are included automatically, and inspection report fees beyond the standard cost. The average inspection fee overcharge is $150.

Understanding Real Estate Fee Risks

The real estate settlement process is governed by the Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act and the Truth in Lending Act, which require specific disclosures and tolerances for closing costs. However, enforcement relies heavily on borrower awareness, which is limited. The three-day review period before closing is often insufficient for homebuyers to verify every charge, especially when they are also coordinating moving logistics, financing contingencies, and inspection resolution. This pressure creates a structural advantage for settlement service providers, who know that most borrowers will sign without question.
