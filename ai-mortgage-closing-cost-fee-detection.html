Why Mortgage Closing Costs Have Hidden Fees

Mortgage closing costs are complex with multiple parties each adding fees. Lenders charge origination fees, discount points, processing fees, and underwriting fees. Third-party providers charge for appraisals, title searches, title insurance, surveys, and credit reports. Settlement agents charge for document preparation, notarization, and recording. Each fee can be inflated independently.

Types of Mortgage Fees AI Detects

Origination Charges

Origination fees of 0.5 to 1 percent of the loan amount are standard. Some lenders charge higher percentages or separate application, processing, and underwriting fees that total more than the standard origination percentage. AI analysis compares origination charges against industry averages for the loan type.

Discount Points

Discount points are prepaid interest that reduces the interest rate. Each point costs 1 percent of the loan amount and typically reduces the rate by 0.25 percent. The value of points depends on how long the borrower keeps the loan. AI analysis calculates the break-even period for discount points.
