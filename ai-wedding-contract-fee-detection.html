What AI Wedding Fee Detection Identifies

Venue Service Charge Confusion

Wedding venues add service charges of 18 to 22 percent that are often described as covering staff costs. AI detects service charges that are applied to the food and beverage minimum before tax and gratuity are added, double-stacking where both a service charge and automatic gratuity are charged, and service charges that are described as discretionary but are mandatory and non-negotiable.

Catering Per-Plate Markups

Catering quotes list a per-plate price that often excludes taxes, service charges, and setup fees. AI detects per-plate prices that exclude mandatory add-ons making the effective cost 25 to 40 percent higher than quoted, children's meal pricing at full adult rates, and vendor meal charges for photographers and musicians that exceed the actual cost of providing those meals.

Photography and Videography Overage Traps

Photography packages include a set number of coverage hours. AI detects overtime rates that are 2 to 3 times the hourly equivalent of the package rate, second shooter charges that apply even when only one photographer is actually assigned, and print release fees that charge for digital files that should be included in the package.

Cancellation and Rescheduling Penalties

Wedding vendor cancellation policies vary dramatically. AI detects cancellation fees that exceed the vendor's actual damages, rescheduling fees that are charged in addition to retained deposits, and force majeure clauses that exclude common events like illness or extreme weather, forcing couples to pay penalties for unavoidable cancellations.

Floral and Decor Markups

Floral arrangements include design fees, delivery charges, setup and breakdown fees. AI detects markup percentages on floral wholesale costs that exceed industry standard ranges of 200 to 300 percent, vase and container rental fees that approach the cost of purchasing those items outright, and preservation or disposal fees that are not disclosed in the original quote.

Music and Entertainment Fee Inflation

Bands and DJs charge for setup time, breaks, and overtime. AI detects setup fees that are charged as a separate line item when they should be included in the performance rate, break time that is included in the contracted hours reducing actual performance time, and overtime rates that are triggered by the vendor's schedule rather than the couple's event timeline.

Alcohol and Bar Service Overcharges

Open bar packages charge per person or per hour. AI detects consumption-based billing that uses inflated per-drink prices compared to standard retail, corkage fees that exceed the actual cost of serving bottles brought by the couple, and bar package minimums that exceed what is reasonably consumed by the expected guest count.

Transportation and Shuttle Service Fees

Wedding transportation contracts include hourly minimums, mileage charges, and gratuity. AI detects minimum hour requirements that exceed the reasonable event duration, mileage overage charges that are not disclosed in the original quote, and automatic gratuity that is added on top of a service charge.
